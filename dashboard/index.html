Token Radar

Unlaunched protocols, ranked by implied value if they launch a token.

What this is

Token Radar ranks 198 unlaunched DeFi protocols by the market cap they would plausibly reach if they launched a token, then prices a concrete farm plan for each one. Implied value is TVL times a fitted size multiple (with an infrastructure intercept), times a growth multiplier from the 7-day TVL change, times a seasoning multiplier, times the fitted probability the protocol ever launches a token. No pre-token haircut is applied: no launched/unlaunched pair in this snapshot can fit a discount, so none is invented. Every number on this page is recomputed live in your browser from the same model — open any row and the dialog shows whether the recompute agrees.

Research note — what changed in v7, and what is still wrong

L1HC — the only model change. The launch-odds model grew from 9 to 12 features with three grouped flags. High-launch category is 1 for Lending, Dexs, Basis Trading or Yield (full-sample launch rates 0.59–0.79). Low-launch category is 1 for Risk Curators, CEX or Onchain Capital Allocator (rates 0.13–0.31). High-rate chain is 1 when any listed chain is Fantom, Mantle, Polygon, Optimism or Base (rates 0.65–0.81). No low-rate-chain flag: it was tested and rejected. The fitted weights are [0.500, 0.172, 0.171, −0.126, −0.010, −0.224, 0.362, 0.187, 0.145, 0.220, −0.520, 0.412], bias 0.233. The new flags carry the largest single weights after age (+0.220 high-category, −0.520 low-category, +0.412 high-rate-chain), and the 5-fold Brier score improved from 0.2030 to 0.1933, holdout 0.1865 against a 0.2476 base rate. The honest train-only cross-validation is 0.1978, so the full-sample 0.1933 is roughly 0.003–0.005 optimistic from group selection.

Calibration improved, not solved. Mean predicted odds against the actual launch rate, by category: Dexs 0.600 vs 0.737 (−0.138) · Lending 0.689 vs 0.787 (−0.098) · Yield 0.504 vs 0.590 (−0.086) · Onchain Capital Allocator 0.229 vs 0.310 (−0.081) · Risk Curators 0.205 vs 0.128 (+0.077) · RWA 0.477 vs 0.515 (−0.038). The worst gap shrank from 0.236 to 0.138, but Dexs is still under-predicted by 0.138 — the model still underestimates the highest-rate categories. Do not read the Brier gain as calibration solved.

Polymarket at 0.824 is one chain effect, not proof. Rank 1 is polymarket-international at $51.89M implied with odds 0.824. Most of that odds jump comes from the Polygon high-rate-chain flag (weight +0.412) plus its category and size — a single chain flag, not fundamental evidence that Polymarket will launch. Treat the rank as a screening signal, not a prediction about one company.

Farm verdicts are slice-sensitive. The standard plan deposits $2,000. At a $1,000 deposit the board has 0 FARM rows; at $5,000 it has 31. The 14 FARM verdicts are a property of the $2,000 slice, not of the protocols alone. Rank 1 polymarket-international is only MARGINAL as a farm (net +$7.92, +$2.93/h) — its TVL is too large for a $2,000 slice to earn a meaningful share. The top farm by net is metalx-swap at +$53.12 (+$19.67/h).

Tested and rejected, not forgotten. L2 interaction terms cannibalised the main effects. L3 strict labelling cut implied value by about 60% and left FARM at 0. L5 TVL-weighted value fitting made the walk-forward miss worse (2.66 to 4.22). Growth as an odds feature did not move the top odds and its weight was unstable. None of these is retested here.

What the backtests actually say. Value: 5-fold CV miss 2.93x against a flat-multiple 5.36x; leave-one-out 2.87x against 5.11x; walk-forward misses 2.66x / 2.73x / 3.26x with P25–P75 hold rates 54.9% / 51.2% / 48.4%. The curve slope bootstrap gives p25 −0.602, p75 −0.471. The model beats a flat multiple everywhere, and still misses by roughly a factor of three out of sample — the range column, not the point estimate, is the honest number.
